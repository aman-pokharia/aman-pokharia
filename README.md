### 💡 About Me

I am an **AI/ML Engineer and Full-Stack Developer** pursuing my B.Tech in Information Technology at **Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology (MAIT)**, New Delhi. 

My work centers on **Agentic AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and scalable backend architectures**. I focus on transforming cutting-edge machine learning research into practical, production-ready applications that solve real-world problems—from autonomous research assistants and agri-tech advisors to community web ecosystems.

- 🤖 **Agentic AI & Autonomy:** Building multi-agent systems with **LangGraph** that leverage autonomous decision-making, dynamic tool invocation, and iterative problem solving.
- 🧠 **Context-Aware GenAI & RAG:** Architecting enterprise retrieval pipelines with vector stores, hybrid semantic search, and open-source models (Mistral, LLaMA).
- ⚡ **Backend & Systems Engineering:** Designing high-throughput, asynchronous REST APIs with **FastAPI** and robust data schemas in **PostgreSQL**.
- 🌐 **Modern Full-Stack Applications:** Developing responsive, high-performance user interfaces using **Next.js**, **React**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**.
- 🧩 **Algorithmic Foundations:** Active member of **Mindflare** at MAIT, continuously practicing Data Structures, Algorithms (DSA), and competitive problem solving.
